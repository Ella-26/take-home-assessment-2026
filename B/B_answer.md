## Note to the partner

Q3 means deals dated 1 Jul to 30 Sep 2026 (announcement date, not closing date).

Two rows count as one deal if company name (ignoring case and suffixes like "Pte Ltd"), round, amount and currency match, and the dates are within 7 days. The 7-day window is our choice. (This merged Kirana Fleet and PayLoom.)

Seed rounds above US$20M and Pre-seed rounds above US$5M are flagged as unusual. These limits are our rule of thumb. Flagged rounds stay in the headline total. Only LedgerLeaf is flagged.
